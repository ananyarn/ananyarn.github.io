About Me

Hi! I’m Ananya Reetha Noble — a Computer Science graduate from Mar Athanasius College of Engineering (APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University), where I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering in 2024 with a CGPA of 9.25. I’m currently pursuing my MTech in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence) at Defence Institute of Advanced Technology (DIAT), Pune.

I enjoy exploring how software can be used to solve practical problems and make technology more inclusive. During my undergrad, I had the chance to work on projects in machine learning, large language models, and software design. One of my key projects involved fine-tuning the Mistral 7B model using LoRA to convert algorithmic steps into working Python code.

I also got involved with NetX, our college’s FOSS Club, which introduced me to the open-source community and taught me the value of collaboration, transparency, and sharing knowledge.

I recently co-authored and published a research paper titled "Exploring LoRA for Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning of LLMs in Enhanced Algorithm-to-Python-Source-Code Translation Task". The paper was presented at the International Conference on Research Advances in Engineering and Technology (ITECHCET 2024) held in Kerala, India on 24th and 25th May 2024, and is published in AIP Conference Proceedings (DOI: 10.1063/5.0247544).

Fun fact: I got into coding by trying to customize a basic website — I’d break the layout, Google the error, dig through forums, and fix it piece by piece. That curiosity led me into the world of open-source, where learning by doing (and debugging!) quickly became my favorite way to grow.
